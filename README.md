# Heater Drill

A Factorio 2.0 mod that adds a mining drill powered by heat instead of electricity.

## Requirements

- Factorio 2.0
- The base game (`base >= 2.0`)

## Crafting

The Heater Drill recipe is available without a technology prerequisite:

- 5 iron gear wheels
- 10 iron plates
- 10 heat pipes

## Using the Heater Drill

Place the drill over a mineable resource patch, then connect a heat network to one of its heat connection points. The connection points are on the south, east, and west sides. The drill requires a heat temperature of at least 100°C.
